# Training Content (Owner Review Only)

This source is excluded from normal candidate context. It is for owner-approved training material only.

## Training Formats

- Training may be written, voice, video, or one-to-one.
- Each application has separate training; training for one application does not complete another application's training.
- Training is free and support is open-ended; a session is usually around one hour but there is no hard time limit.
- No exam is required after a candidate says they understand.

## Message, Bio, and Gift Banks

- Message, bio, and gift examples remain owner-review material.
- The 100-message/bio bank must not enter the active candidate knowledge context automatically.
- A future activation requires separate owner approval and a training-only publish manifest.

## Training Handoff

- Training starts only after installation and agency evidence are approved.
- The current educator is selected dynamically by owner-approved application-specific information.
- Unresolved training questions may be explained again or routed to the current educator.
